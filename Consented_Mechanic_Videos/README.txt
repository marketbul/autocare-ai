Drop any consented / permitted mechanic or car-owner MP4 videos into this folder.
Then double-click run_auto_collector.bat in the project root folder.
It will automatically:
1. Extract 16kHz audio WAV
2. Transcribe speech to text (when faster-whisper is installed)
3. Capture JPG screenshots at exact spoken damage timestamps
4. Clean and export everything into AI_Auto_Pakistan_Dataset\dataset_viewer.html
